The studio

An independent salon in a Victorian shopfront.

Nic Hau works from 105 Church Street, in a row of two-storey Victorian shops near the corner of Kent Street. The strip is part of the Church Street North heritage precinct, one of Richmond’s earliest shopping streets.

Bring a photo, or bring nothing but a feeling about your hair. Thick hair, curls, fine hair, long layers and sharp short shapes are all welcome in the chair.

  • Women’s, men’s and children’s cuts
  • Colour planned around the cut
  • Unhurried appointments
  • Book online or by phone
